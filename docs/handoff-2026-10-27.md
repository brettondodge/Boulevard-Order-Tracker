## What's finished

### Fixes and Undo
- The "Preview data" label is gone. Your Google email shows top right, and
  every change records who made it.

- Delete replaces Archive for orders and approved jobs (hidden, not erased,

  shorter rows, compact phone cards.

### The order flow
- **PO** sits next to the customer: the reference you put on the supplier
  order.
- **Mark received** asks which shelf the material is on.
- Steps: Ordered → Received → Scheduled → Complete → Invoiced → Archived.
  **Done** = complete with no invoice sent; **Invoiced** = an unpaid invoice
  sent; **Archived** = complete and paid in full, deleted 7 days later
  (added October 28).
- Tabs for the labor meeting: **To schedule** (here, not scheduled),
  **Scheduled**, **Done**, **Invoiced**, **Archived**. Each line shows its
  next step, and warns when a job is scheduled before its material arrived.
- Each order stands on its own.
- **Start fresh** (Master Lists, admin) clears orders and approved jobs, and
  optionally suppliers and staff, for go-live.
- The Google Sheet import was removed, as you asked.

### Warranty (added October 28)
- **Warranty** tab, after Approved – Need to Order, with **Add warranty**.
  Each warranty has the customer, PO, address, line items (typed by staff),
  what's wrong and what's needed.
- Two HCP boxes: **Original job** (reference; pasting its link fills the
  customer and address) and **Warranty job** (optional; the new HCP job for
  the warranty, whose schedule, completion and Pipeline column the orders
  follow).
- **Order materials** works as on an approved job, with no deposit: one order
  marks the warranty all ordered, or Save + another supplier. The PO and
  notes (what's needed and the line items) fill in.
- The orders show in every tab (To schedule and on) with a **Warranty** badge,
  and are listed on the warranty with their step.
- Warranties are never deleted automatically. Their orders are archived and
  deleted after 7 days like any other.

### Housecall Pro (see `docs/housecall-pro.md`)
- **Paste an HCP job link (or job #)** on an order or approved job. The
  customer, job #, category (from the HCP job type) and notes fill in. On a
  warranty, the customer and address.
- **Tags and notes go to HCP:** "Materials ordered" when ordered, "Materials
  received" when everything is here, plus a note for each order and delivery
  (with PO and shelf).
- **Scheduled and complete come from HCP** within seconds (webhooks), with an
  hourly catch-up. Linked lines get a "Schedule in HCP" button.
- **Pipeline Jobs board (added October 28):** the tracker moves each job
  forward through Deposit Needed, Need to Order Materials, Waiting on
  Materials and Materials Received. Admins pick the columns in Master Lists,
  and there's a Try one job button. Pipeline is being kept.
- Not used, as you asked: auto-added approved jobs, payment details,
  tech names, HCP's own deposits, the canceled flag, staff list.
- Admins choose which tracker category each HCP job type fills in (Master
  Lists › Housecall Pro).
