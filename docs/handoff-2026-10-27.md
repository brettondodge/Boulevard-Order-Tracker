# Order Tracker handoff — October 27, 2026

Everything below is on the **`tracker-updates`** branch on GitHub (latest
commit `96bb20a`). None of it is live yet: the live service still runs `codex/production-preparation` at
`e9339d7` (Freeport). Going live needs your OK to merge, then a manual deploy
in Render, following the project's usual deploy rule.

## What's finished

### 1. Fixes and Undo (commit 82d734d)
- The "Preview data" label is gone. Your Google email is shown top right, and
  every change records who made it.
- Undo bar for 15 seconds after any add, edit, receive, reopen or delete.
- Delete replaces Archive for orders and approved jobs. A deleted record is
  hidden, not erased, which is what makes Undo possible.
- Fixes: blanking a field on an approved job now saves; an older screen can't
  overwrite a newer save; no database details in error messages.
- Filters fit one row on a computer and sit behind a Filters button on
  phones, with checkbox dropdowns. Rows are shorter, and phone cards are compact.

### 2. Job progress for the labor meeting (de384ac, d04e764, f955255)
- Each order has a shop location, scheduled (date and note), job complete
  and invoiced. Each order keeps its own, like a row in the sheet (changed
  Oct 27 at your request; orders used to be grouped into jobs, which showed
  "0 of 2 here").
- New tabs: **To schedule** (received and not scheduled), **Scheduled** and
  **Need invoice**. Each line shows its step and a next-step button (Mark
  received → Mark scheduled → Mark complete → Mark invoiced). It also warns
  when a job is scheduled before its material arrived.
- Condensed lines (your labor-meeting view) gained Shop and Schedule note
  columns, and fits a 1440-pixel-wide screen.
- Approved – Need to Order now lists only jobs still needing materials, with
  a button to show the rest.
- **Import from Google Sheet** (Master Lists, admin): preview first, then
  Add or Replace. Tried on your real sheet locally: **146 orders** (68 window,
  78 glass), 9 suppliers and 9 people. 23 rows that were complete and billed
  were left out. 60 orders picked up their HCP job number from the invoice
  column.
- Fixed after an independent review: 23 confirmed findings (15 distinct problems). The most important:
  saving one order could overwrite a sibling's completion or invoice.

### 3. Housecall Pro connection (308b338, d0a0bdd, and the review fixes after)
See `docs/housecall-pro.md` for what it does and the setup steps.
- Webhooks update scheduled, complete and invoiced within seconds, an hourly
  sync catches anything missed, and approved estimates appear as approved
  jobs.
- Built against HCP's official API spec and webhook docs. Tested against a
  fake HCP server, never the real account.
- A second review flagged 9 findings. Its fact-checkers hit a usage limit, so
  I checked each against the code myself, then fixed all 9 and 2 more of my own:
  - replays of a captured webhook;
  - two deliveries at once overwriting each other;
  - jobs that aren't in the tracker being stored;
  - finished and billed history being reopened;
  - controls staying locked after HCP is switched off;
  - one bad job stopping the whole sync;
  - rate-limit timing;
  - free-text job IDs;
  - a problem message that never cleared;
  - legacy linked orders never linking;
  - unrelated jobs costing API calls.

## What needs you

   `codex/production-preparation` and push. You then deploy in Render
   (auto-deploy is off). Migrations 006–008 run automatically.
2. **Before importing the sheet:** trigger the backup job in Render
   (boulevard-tools-daily-backup › Trigger run) so there's a fresh backup.
3. **Import:** download the sheet again (File › Download › Microsoft Excel).
   Then Master Lists › Import from Google Sheet › Preview. Choose **Replace**,
   because the tracker holds about 280 test orders, 78 test jobs and 104 test
   suppliers. Type REPLACE.
4. **After importing, in Master Lists:**
   - rename the staff initials to full names (RD, AD, BD, SD, KA, JC, MN, KL,
     plus "Unknown" for rows with no name);
   - give full names to supplier codes I couldn't decode (CW, OBE, CRL);
   - check rows under "Unknown supplier (from sheet)".

   Renaming keeps every link.
5. **Glass tab categories:** imported glass orders are filed under Showers
   (Arcadia under Storefront). Change any that are Flat Glass or Storefront.
6. **Housecall Pro** (steps in `docs/housecall-pro.md`):
   - an HCP admin creates a read-only API key;
   - add `HCP_API_KEY` in Render;
   - add the Cloudflare Bypass rule for `/api/hcp/webhooks`;
   - deploy;
   - turn on HCP Webhooks with the URL;
   - add `HCP_WEBHOOK_SECRET` in Render;
   - press Sync now;
   - optionally turn on HCP's "Automatically copy an approved estimate to a
     new job".
7. **Cancel the Pipeline add-on** once you're happy. Nothing uses it.

  has a Received box.
